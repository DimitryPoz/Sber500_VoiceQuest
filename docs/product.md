# Product

## Problem

Traditional language exercises often feel repetitive and do not give children a strong reason to speak out loud.

## Solution

VoiceQuest makes speaking the main game mechanic. Children act as characters and recreate short scenes in a foreign language.

## Core experience

1. Pick a character.
2. Pick a scene.
3. Listen to a line.
4. Record the line.
5. Receive pronunciation and fluency feedback.
6. Repeat if useful.
7. Earn XP.
8. Unlock the next line or scene.

## MVP content

2–3 original characters  
5 short scenes  
30–50 dialogue lines

Original characters are preferred for the MVP to avoid licensing dependencies.
